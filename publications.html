"Real-Time Vehicle Detection and Enumeration Using Unmanned Aerial Systems"

A. Khalid, O. Ahmed, and E. Debeb

Unmanned Aerial Systems (UAS) equipped with high-resolution cameras have become powerful tools for large-scale traffic monitoring, infrastructure assessment, and urban planning. This research presents a real-time vehicle detection and counting framework that integrates the YOLOv12 object detection model with aerial imagery acquired from a UAS platform, fine-tuned on the VisDrone dataset to handle varying altitudes, lighting conditions, and dense urban scenes. Experimental testing demonstrated detection accuracy and frame-rate performance suitable for real-time traffic analytics and aerial surveillance applications.
