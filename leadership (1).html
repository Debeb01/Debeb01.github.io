Four seasons on FIRST Tech Challenge Team 1002 Surge in Marietta, GA, in two roles: designing and building robot subsystems, and leading the team’s outreach. Each example below follows the STAR format: Situation, Task, Action, Result.

Section 1

Technical Deep Dive

Design Lead & CAD Builder · SOLIDWORKS, 3D printing, Python robot programming

ZeroTolerance: Drive Base Design

Centerstage · 2023–24 season · View CAD
Situation

The 2023–24 Centerstage season. Every other subsystem on the robot is built on top of its drive base, so the drivetrain had to be right early.

Task

Design the drive base (drivetrain), the chassis and drive system for ZeroTolerance.

Action
  • Modeled the chassis and drive system in SOLIDWORKS.
  • Used 3D printing for rapid prototyping and ran build, test, and rebuild cycles against season deadlines.
  • Worked with software teammates so the hardware integrated cleanly with robot code.
Result
  • West Georgia League: Inspire Award and Winning Alliance (1st pick).
  • Georgia State Championship: 3rd Inspire.
  • Qualified for the FIRST Championship.

Chipi: Claw End-Effector Design

Into the Deep · 2024–25 season · View CAD
Situation

The 2024–25 Into the Deep season, where scoring depended on how reliably the robot could grip and handle game elements.

Task

Design the claw, the end-effector that picks up and places game elements on Chipi.

Action
  • Designed the claw in SOLIDWORKS and prototyped parts with 3D printing.
  • Mentored underclassmen in CAD, build accuracy, and troubleshooting.
  • Served as stand-in drive coach, analyzing opposing robots and coaching the driver on real-time scoring decisions.
Result
  • Athens–Marietta League: Inspire Award and Finalist Alliance Captain.
  • Chicago Robotics Invitational: Premier selection.
  • Competed at the Georgia State Championship.
