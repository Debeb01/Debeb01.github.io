Title

Developing a Life Jacket Delivery System for UAVs

Abstract

The purpose of this study was to develop and evaluate a lightweight life jacket delivery system using UAVs, intending to improve response times in drowning emergencies. Existing drowning prevention technologies, such as CCTV surveillance and traditional flotation delivery technologies, are limited by their mobility and have difficulty reaching victims in remote and hard-to-reach areas. To address these gaps, this research designed a servo-actuated payload release mechanism to securely carry and accurately deploy flotation devices from a multi-rotor UAV. The mechanism went through several design iterations modeled in SOLIDWORKS. The final design was selected based on weight, expected performance, and durability, then tested with a payload. The final design utilized a linkage-based latch with an MG996R torque servo and successfully released a life jacket in the flight test. To estimate the mechanism’s carrying capacity, Finite Element Analysis was performed on the final CAD assembly, indicating that the linkage bar reached its allowable stress at a load of 6.506 kg, below the original 25 kg target. The results support further development of UAV-based flotation delivery systems, while showing that the mechanism would need redesign to meet the original 25 kg target.
