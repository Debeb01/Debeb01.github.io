Senior Capstone Project

Life Jacket Delivery System

Servo-actuated payload release for multirotor UAVs

Designed to carry and release a flotation device from a multirotor UAV to shorten response times in drowning emergencies, particularly in remote areas that CCTV surveillance and conventional flotation delivery can't reach. The design went through several SOLIDWORKS iterations, and the final version was chosen on weight, estimated performance, and estimated durability.

The final design uses a linkage-based latch driven by an MG996R torque servo. It was bench-tested with a payload and successfully released a life jacket in flight testing. Finite element analysis on the final assembly found the linkage bar reached its allowable stress at a 6.506 kg load, below the hypothesized 25 kg carrying capacity, which points to where the next iteration should be strengthened.
