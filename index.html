About Me

I am a first-year engineering student at Purdue University, and I plan to major in aerospace engineering. I am drawn to unmanned aircraft systems and mechanical design, where an idea only counts once it has been modeled, tested and flown.

As an aerospace research intern at the Kennesaw State University AERO Lab, I contributed to a UAV-based real-time vehicle detection framework presented at the 2026 AIAA AVIATION Forum. For my senior capstone, I designed and flight-tested a servo-actuated life jacket delivery mechanism for multirotor UAVs, refining it through CAD modeling and finite element analysis.

On FIRST Tech Challenge Team 1002 Surge, I served as lead designer, marketing lead and drive coach across four seasons, including three FIRST World Championship appearances. I want to bring that same build-test-improve approach to an internship, co-op or REU in aerospace or mechanical engineering.
