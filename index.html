About

Ethan is a first-year aerospace engineering student at Purdue University, drawn to the intersection of unmanned aircraft systems and mechanical design — where an idea only counts once it has been modeled, tested, and flown. That approach was shaped by two years of iterating real hardware under real constraints: refining a UAV payload-release mechanism through CAD and finite element analysis until the numbers held up, and leading a robotics subsystem through four competitive seasons of build-test-rebuild cycles under strict deadlines.

As an aerospace research intern at the Kennesaw State University AERO Lab, Ethan contributed to a UAV-based real-time vehicle detection framework presented at the 2026 AIAA Aviation Forum, and independently designed and flight-tested a servo-actuated life jacket delivery mechanism for multirotor UAVs as his senior capstone project. On FIRST Tech Challenge Team 1002 Surge (CircuitRunners Robotics), he has served as marketing lead, robot designer, and drive coach across four seasons that reached the FIRST World Championship in 2023, 2024, and 2026.

Ethan is now looking to bring that same build-test-iterate approach to a summer internship or Research Experience for Undergraduates (REU) in aerospace or mechanical engineering.
