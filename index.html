About Me

I am a first-year engineering student at Purdue University planning to major in aerospace engineering. I am especially interested in unmanned aircraft systems and mechanical design, and I enjoy taking a design from CAD through analysis, testing and iteration.

At the Kennesaw State University AERO Lab, I worked on UAV research involving vehicle detection and enumeration and contributed to research presented at the 2026 AIAA AVIATION Forum. My high school senior capstone focused on a servo-actuated life jacket delivery mechanism for a multirotor UAV, which I modeled in SOLIDWORKS, evaluated with finite element analysis and flight-tested.

Before Purdue, I spent four years with FIRST Tech Challenge Team 1002 Surge as a lead designer, CAD builder, marketing lead and drive coach. Those experiences taught me to troubleshoot under pressure, communicate design decisions and keep improving a system after the first version works.
